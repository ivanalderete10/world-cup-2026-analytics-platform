# SQL Practice Exercises

## Beginner
1. List the 48 World Cup 2026 teams by group.
2. Count historical matches by tournament.
3. Find every match played by Argentina.
4. Show the top 10 live Power Score teams.

## Intermediate
5. Calculate win, draw and loss rates by team.
6. Retrieve the latest ELO rating per team using a window function.
7. Compare two teams head-to-head.
8. Rank confederations by average Power Score.

## Advanced
9. Build a rolling 10-match form score.
10. Compare ELO rank with Power Score rank.
11. Find teams whose predictive ranking most exceeds their ELO ranking.
12. Create a stored procedure returning the most probable scorelines for any pair of teams.
